The short version

FinanceOS does not collect your data. There is no account, no signup, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device. The only times the app talks to the internet are to fetch public market data (stock prices and exchange rates) — and those requests contain nothing personal about you.

What we do NOT collect

We collect zero personal data. We never collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or contact details
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers (no IDFA, IDFV, or advertising IDs)
  • Financial account numbers, card numbers, or bank credentials — FinanceOS never connects to your bank
  • Your balances, transactions, budgets, goals, or anything you type into the app
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral data
  • Anything for advertising

There is no server on our end that could receive this information — the app is fully client-side.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never uploaded, synced to a cloud, or shared. Delete the app and your data is deleted with it.

  • Backups: you can export a .json file from the app's menu. Exported files are not encrypted — store them somewhere safe.
  • No cloud backup: FinanceOS does not back up to iCloud or any other service. Your export file is your backup.

PIN lock and encryption

With PIN lock on, your on-device data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your PIN on your device (PBKDF2, 150,000 iterations) and never leaves it. We cannot see, reset, or recover your PIN — forget it and the encrypted data cannot be recovered by anyone.

Network requests the app makes

FinanceOS works fully offline. It contacts the internet only for public market data, and no request includes anything personal:

  • Exchange rates — daily ECB rates via the free Frankfurter API (api.frankfurter.dev). The request contains only currency codes (e.g. USD → MXN).
  • Stock & ETF quotes — Yahoo Finance data through a public CORS proxy (corsproxy.io, fallback api.allorigins.win). The request contains only the ticker symbols you asked to update (e.g. AAPL, VOO) — never holdings, balances, or identity. As with any internet request, the proxy operator technically sees your IP address with the tickers, but nothing identifying you or your finances.
  • Optional: your own free Finnhub API key (Settings) fetches quotes directly from Finnhub instead. The key stays on your device.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to access or correct on any server. Your rights are simple:

  • See everything: it's all in the app, on your device.
  • Delete everything: erase data in Settings, or delete the app.
  • Take it with you: export a .json backup any time.

Children

FinanceOS collects no data from anyone, including children. No ads, no chat, no social features.
