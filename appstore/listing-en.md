## Description

FinanceOS — your complete financial command center.

See your entire financial life in one private place. FinanceOS tracks your accounts, credit cards, investments, income, and budgets — and teaches you the "why" behind every number with bite-size interactive lessons.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no signup, no tracking, no ads
• Your data lives only on your device — we have no servers to send it to
• Optional PIN lock with AES-256 encryption
• One-tap Privacy Mode blurs every amount on screen

TODAY — YOUR FINANCIAL PULSE
A Financial Health score (0–100) that rolls cash flow, debt, safety net, and growth into one honest number. Plus a "needs attention" feed, net worth with daily change, upcoming deposits, and one-tap expense logging.

ACCOUNTS THAT DO THE MATH
Checking, savings, and investment accounts with automatic interest — daily, monthly, quarterly, or fixed-term (CDs, Cetes, plazo fijo). Interest is credited on schedule, net of tax, so your net worth is always current.

CREDIT CARDS WITHOUT SURPRISES
Statement-cut and payment-due countdowns with alerts, utilization bars, and high-balance warnings — never miss a due date again.

PORTFOLIO WITH LIVE PRICES
Stocks and ETFs with live quotes and dividends (no API key needed), allocation and return-by-position charts, after-tax returns, and per-position price history.

INCOME, MAPPED
Salaries and recurring income with gross/net and tax handling, a 30-day deposit timeline, and a 12-month net projection.

BUDGETING WITHOUT THE DATA ENTRY
Import your bank or card statement PDF right on your device (beta: Amex, Klar, Openbank, Santander) — or upload our spreadsheet template. Get a spending-health score, a 50/30/20 breakdown, per-category budgets, and month-over-month trends.

GOALS & MILESTONES
Set savings goals, earn 19 achievements for healthy money habits, and see your estimated global wealth percentile to stay motivated.

LEARN BY PLAYING
Four interactive scenario games — paychecks, credit cards, market crashes, inflation — plus Wealth Builder, a 20-year investing sandbox. Every choice shows its 10-year impact. Earn XP as you learn.

BUILT FOR THE REAL WORLD
• English and Spanish (español mexicano)
• 30 currencies with daily ECB exchange rates
• Works offline — only price and rate refreshes need internet
• Free. No in-app purchases, no subscriptions

FinanceOS never moves your money and never connects to your bank — it's your private ledger, calculator, and coach in one.

## Review notes for the App Review team
Thank you for reviewing FinanceOS!

• No login or demo account is needed — the app opens straight into the dashboard.
• To explore with realistic data, tap "Load sample data" on the welcome screen (also available in Settings).
• All features work offline. Only two things need internet: live stock quotes (Portfolio → "Update prices") and exchange-rate refreshes. Everything else — accounts, cards, budgets, lessons, milestones — works fully offline.
• Optional PIN lock is in Settings; reviewers may set any PIN to test the lock screen and encrypted storage.
• FinanceOS is a personal ledger and calculator: it never moves money, never connects to bank accounts, and provides no personalized financial advice. The Learn games and Wealth Builder use hypothetical scenarios; global wealth percentiles are rough motivational estimates from public datasets, disclosed in the app.
• Free app. No in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads, no account, no tracking.
